## FAQ

**Q: Is this pack compatible with older versions of Substance 3D Painter, like 2024 or 2025?**
A: While the pack is designed and tested for the 2026 version, many of the core smart materials and textures use standard Substance file formats (.sbsar, .sppr) and may load in older versions. However, some advanced generators or features that rely on the 2026 engine might not function correctly or could cause performance issues. It is strongly recommended to use it with the specified version for the best experience.

**Q: The materials appear low-quality or don't render properly. How do I troubleshoot this?**
A: First, ensure your graphics drivers are up to date. Substance 3D Painter relies heavily on GPU acceleration. If the issue persists, try switching the viewport rendering mode from "Iray" to "Painting" or "Fast" mode to see if it's a real-time viewport issue. Finally, go to `Edit > Settings > Performance` and try resetting the cache or adjusting the VRAM usage slider.

**Q: Can I use these assets in my commercial projects or games?**
A: Yes. All materials and textures included in this pack are royalty-free and can be used for both personal and commercial projects, including games, films, and illustrations. You are free to modify them to suit your needs.
